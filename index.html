Positioning

Independent judgement at the boundary of law, markets and technology.

Institutional digital-asset and market-infrastructure projects often fail in the gaps between legal design, product architecture and technical implementation. McGonagle Advisory works at that boundary, helping senior teams design products and systems that are commercially coherent, legally grounded and operationally viable.

Legal intent into system design Rights, obligations and documentation logic translated into product architecture and operating models.
Institutional tokenised workflows Tokenised products and smart contract workflows structured for regulated, capital markets use.
Independent challenge Senior review where strategy, architecture, delivery and client expectations need to align.

Services

Advisory services for complex digital market infrastructure work.

Engagements are scoped around clear decisions, deliverables and senior stakeholder needs — from strategy through product architecture and independent design review.

Strategic advisory

Direction, structure, market entry.

Advising leadership teams on product direction, market infrastructure design and digital asset strategy. This includes tokenisation strategy, structuring tokenised financial products, and developing new product models within existing legal, regulatory and market frameworks.

Typical outputs Board briefings, strategy papers, product direction, market-entry analysis.

Product and system design

From documentation to data to lifecycle.

Defining how financial products are represented in data, system logic and operating models. This includes lifecycle modelling, documentation-to-data mapping, smart contract logic and tokenised product architectures.

Typical outputs Operating models, product architecture, data models, lifecycle workflows.

Independent review

Stress-tested before capital and delivery are committed.

Reviewing proposed products, platforms and architectures to test whether legal rights, obligations, lifecycle events and operational dependencies are accurately reflected before significant capital or delivery effort is committed.

Typical outputs Design reviews, risk assessments, investor or client-readiness reviews.

McGonagle Advisory does not provide formal legal or regulatory advice, software development, implementation delivery or staff augmentation. Where legal, regulatory, tax or technical advice is required, the relevant specialist advisers should be engaged.

Approach

Practical engagement formats for senior teams.

Engagements can take the form of focused decision support, structured diagnostics or ongoing senior input into live programmes.

Executive briefing

1–2 sessions over 1–2 weeks

A focused session for boards, founders or leadership teams assessing a product, market-structure opportunity or strategic direction.

Targeted diagnostic

3–6 weeks, fixed scope, fixed fee

A short, structured review of a proposed product, platform or workflow, identifying design gaps, execution risks and decision points.

Embedded advisory

Monthly retainer, multi-quarter engagements

Senior advisory support for live programmes where legal, product, commercial and technical teams need a common architecture and narrative.
